Join the Team

Our church volunteers play a vital role in serving the congregation and community, fostering a sense of belonging, understanding, and connection with the wider community. Through their dedication, kindness, and willingness to help, they create a welcoming environment where people can grow in faith and fellowship. Whether assisting during worship services, supporting outreach programs, helping with children's ministry, or serving behind the scenes, every volunteer makes a meaningful difference. Their service reflects Christ's love and strengthens the mission of the church.

We believe that everyone has unique gifts and talents that can be used to glorify God and bless others. Volunteering provides an opportunity to build relationships, develop skills, and make a lasting impact in the lives of those around us. No matter your experience or availability, there is a place for you to serve. Join our team of dedicated volunteers as we work together to share hope, encourage one another, and advance God's kingdom in our community.


Volunteer With Us Through the Following Ministries

Ushering & Security.

Help create a safe, welcoming environment for everyone who walks through our doors. Ushers assist guests, provide directions, and support the flow of services, while security volunteers help maintain a secure and peaceful atmosphere for the congregation and visitors alike.

Sunday School.

Serve the next generation by helping children learn about God in a fun, engaging, and caring environment. Volunteers assist with lessons, activities, and supervision, helping children grow in faith and build meaningful connections within the church community.

Praise & Worship.

Use your musical gifts to lead the congregation in worship. Whether singing, playing an instrument, or supporting rehearsals, the praise and worship team helps create an atmosphere that encourages people to connect with God through praise and worship.

Intercession Team.

Stand with others in prayer and offer encouragement to those seeking spiritual support. Intercession team volunteers help create opportunities for people to experience comfort, hope, and God's presence through intentional and compassionate prayer.

Choir

Join a passionate group of singers dedicated to leading and enhancing worship through song. Choir members minister to the congregation through special performances, choir festivals, and church events while growing in musical excellence and spiritual unity

General Administrative Support.

Support the ministries and events of the church by providing practical assistance wherever it is needed. Volunteers in this team work alongside ministry leaders and planners, offering their time, skills, and manpower to help activities run smoothly. Whether setting up venues, assisting with event coordination, distributing materials, helping with logistics, or simply being an extra pair of hands, this team plays a vital role in supporting the work of the church. No special expertise is required, just a willing heart to serve and a readiness to help wherever needed.

Technical & Stage Management.

Support services by managing audio, lighting, visuals, and stage operations. Technical volunteers work behind the scenes to ensure presentations, worship, and events run smoothly, creating an excellent experience for everyone attending

Media & Informatics.

Help communicate the church's vision through technology, media, and information management. Volunteers serve in areas such as videography, live streaming, social media, website management, graphic design, ensuring that the church remains connected, informed, and equipped for effective ministry both online and in person.

Hospitality & Decorations.

Provide a warm and welcoming environment for all guests and members. Volunteers in this team are responsible for setting up and managing hospitality areas, creating beautiful decorations, and ensuring that everyone feels valued and cared for during church events and gatherings.
