Cell Groups

Home Cell Groups are small gatherings that meet regularly in homes to encourage spiritual growth, fellowship, prayer, and discipleship. They provide an opportunity for members to build meaningful relationships, study God's Word together, support one another through life's challenges, and grow in their faith in a more personal setting. In a large congregation, cell groups help create a sense of belonging and ensure that no one walks their spiritual journey alone.

Home cell groups are deeply rooted in the example of the early church. Believers met together in homes to pray, learn from the apostles' teachings, break bread, and strengthen one another in faith. These gatherings played a vital role in building strong Christian communities and spreading the Gospel. Today, our cell groups continue this biblical model by creating spaces where people can connect, serve, and mature spiritually while remaining actively involved in the life and mission of the church.
