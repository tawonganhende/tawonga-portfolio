We Are God's Grace Center

AFM in Zimbabwe God’s Grace Centre is a vibrant Pentecostal assembly committed to elevating believers for excellence and impact. Rooted in the Word of God and empowered by the Holy Spirit, we exist to bring transformation in our community through worship, discipleship, and service.

Our Purpose.

Our purpose is to glorify God by leading people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ. We aim to nurture spiritual growth, strengthen families, and empower individuals to live out their faith in everyday life. At God’s Grace Centre, we believe in raising a Christ-centered generation that influences society with integrity, love, and excellence.

Our Mission.

Our mission is to be a beacon of hope in Warren Park and beyond. We are dedicated to:

  • Preaching the gospel of salvation through Jesus Christ.
  • Equipping believers through teaching, prayer, and fellowship.
  • Promoting holiness and the baptism of the Holy Spirit.
  • Building strong communities through outreach, youth empowerment, and charitable initiatives.
  • Inspiring excellence in every sphere of life — spiritual, social, and professional.

Our Values.

Identity in Christ

We believe that every believer finds true identity in Jesus Christ. At God’s Grace Centre, we encourage members to grow in faith, live by the Word, and walk in the power of the Holy Spirit

Church Is Family

We are more than a congregation, we are a family. Our fellowship is built on love, unity, and support, where everyone is valued and nurtured to reach their God-given potential.

Kingdom Culture

We strive to cultivate a culture that reflects the Kingdom of God. This means living lives of holiness, integrity, and service, while impacting our community with excellence and compassion.

Spirit Led Life

We are committed to living Spirit-led lives. Through prayer, worship, and obedience to the Holy Spirit, we seek guidance, empowerment, and transformation in every aspect of life.


What We Believe.

Salvation in Jesus Christ

We believe that salvation comes only through faith in Jesus Christ, who died and rose again for our redemption. Every believer is called to accept Him as Lord and Savior and live a transformed life.

Authority of the Bible

We hold the Bible as the inspired Word of God and the final authority for faith, doctrine, and conduct. It is the foundation of our teaching, worship, and daily living.

Empowerment by the Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, evidenced by spiritual gifts such as speaking in tongues. The Spirit empowers believers for holy living, service, and effective witness.

Holiness and Evangelism

We are committed to living holy lives and spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ. Our mission is to reach communities with the message of salvation and demonstrate God’s love through service and compassion.


Meet Our Team.

L. Mhereyenyoka Senior Pastor

Provides spiritual leadership and vision for the church, overseeing its ministries, teaching God's Word, and guiding the congregation in its mission. He is responsible for pastoral care, discipleship, and ensuring the church remains grounded in biblical principles and Christ-centered service.

P. Mhereyenyoka Pastor's Wife & Ladies Leader

C. Zimunya Graduate Pastor

C. Kanyenge Graduate Pastor & Worship Team Leader

Elder Mangami Chairman of the Board of Elders
